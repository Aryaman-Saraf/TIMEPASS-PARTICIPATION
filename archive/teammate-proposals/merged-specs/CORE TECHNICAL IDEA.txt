CORE TECHNICAL IDEA
The Core AI/ML Modules1. Temporal Audio-Visual SynchronizationThe platform synchronizes candidate utterances with their physical posture to distinguish natural pauses from off-screen reading:Audio Ingestion: Audio is buffered into chunks and processed via an automatic speech recognition (ASR) pipeline with word-level timestamps (e.g., OpenAI Whisper or Google Speech-to-Text). This outputs the precise interval $[t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}]$ for every uttered word and hesitation pause.Visual Pose & Gaze Tracking: The video pipeline samples webcam frames at 15–30 FPS using MediaPipe Face Mesh and Iris Tracking. It calculates:Head Pose Estimation: SolvePnP mapping 2D facial landmarks to a 3D canonical face model to extract Euler angles (Yaw, Pitch, Roll).Gaze Deviation Vector: Euclidean distance ratio of iris center coordinates relative to eye corner anchors.Time-Window Alignment ($\Delta t$): The system cross-references video gaze deviation events with audio timestamps. If gaze deviation exceeds a baseline threshold ($\text{Yaw} > 25^\circ$ or sustained off-screen fixation) concurrently with long pauses ($>2.5\text{s}$) or fluent technical monologue, the frame interval is flagged as an Attention Anomaly.2. Semantic & Technical Relevancy PipelineRather than relying on basic string matching, the evaluation runs on dense semantic vector spaces:Triple-Embedding Alignment: Three distinct embeddings are generated:Requirement Vector ($V_R$): Key skill clusters extracted from the recruiter's Job Description.Resume Claim Vector ($V_C$): Specific project and skill claims extracted from the parsed candidate PDF.Response Vector ($V_A$): The candidate's spoken response text.Cosine Similarity & Knowledge Coverage: The engine calculates directional cosine similarity between $V_A$ and $V_R$, weighted by whether the question probed a claimed skill in $V_C$.Hallucination / Vagueness Penalty: If the candidate uses circular phrasing without domain-specific entity density (measured via Named Entity Recognition for technical frameworks, tools, and methodologies), the system reduces the technical depth score.3. Acoustic & Delivery QuantificationCadence & Hesitation: Calculation of words-per-minute (WPM) variance and ratio of pause duration to vocalized speech.Filler Word Density: Frequency analysis of non-lexical tokens ("um", "uh", "like") normalized per 100 words uttered.4. The Decision Methodology: Explainable Evidence DossierThe platform explicitly avoids arbitrary "Accept" or "Reject" classifications, which are legally problematic and prone to algorithmic bias. Instead, it compiles an Explainable Evidence Dossier:Technical Relevancy Index (0–100): Semantic alignment between questions and domain-specific answers.Articulation & Delivery Index (0–100): Speech continuity, structure, and pacing.Integrity Confidence Score (0–100): Percentage of interview time spent maintaining active screen focus during answer generation.Timestamped Evidence Reel: Recruiter can click any flagged anomaly or low-scoring answer to inspect the exact 10-second synchronized video clip and transcript segment.
